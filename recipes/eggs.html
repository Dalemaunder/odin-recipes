Eggs

How the eggs will look when cooking

Ingredients

  • As many eggs as you'd like to eat
  • Oil

Instructions

  1. Pre-heat the pan on medium-high,
  2. Splash some water on the pan, if the water skitters across the surface then it's ready,
  3. Add oil to the pan; enough to coat the surface,
  4. Crack the eggs into the pan, or a bowl first if you're not confident with cracking eggs,
  5. Cover the pan until they're cooked, idk how long lmao,
  6. Serve hot, possibly on toast, or maybe a steak from one of our other recipes.
