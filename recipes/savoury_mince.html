Savoury Mince

The finished result, good enough to eat

Ingredients

  • 500g of lean beef mince
  • Oil
  • Salt
  • Water
  • Plain flour
  • Gravox powder
  • Bag of frozen mixed vegetables (peas, carrots, corn)
  • Small onion
  • Oyster sauce
  • Sesame oil

Instructions

  1. Add two tablespoons of gravox powder and two tablespoons of flour to 200ml of water, mix and let it sit,
  2. Pre-heat the pan on medium-high to high,
  3. Finely chop the onion,
  4. Add a drizzle of oil to the pan and let it heat,
  5. Saute the onion until translucent, remove and transfer to a bowl
  6. Brown the mince, brown in batches if needed; add to same bowl as onions if doing batches
  7. Pour the gravox/flour mix to the pan to deglaze and let simmer,
  8. Once beginning to thicken, re-add all mince and onions,
  9. Add a good dollop of oyster sauce and a drizzle of sesame oil, stir,
  10. Add a large amount of frozen vegetables to the ,
  11. Allow the vegetables to thaw and come up to heat, then remove from the stove,
  12. Serve in a bowl while hot.
