Steak

How the steak should look when it's ready

Ingredients

  • One 200-300g scotch fillet
  • A drizzle of high-temp neutral oil
  • Table salt
  • 20g salted butter
  • One sprig of rosemary
  • One slice of hearty bread (sourdough, tiger loaf, etc.)

Instructions

  1. Pre-heat the pan on high,
  2. Remove the steak from its packaging and pat dry with a paper towel,
  3. Once very hot, add a drizzle of oil to the pan and let it coat the area the steak will sit,
  4. Sprinkle the steak with salt, then lay the side down on the oiled area of the pan,
  5. If you have a suitable weight, place it on top of the steak to keep the under side in contact with the pan,
  6. Leave the steak to sear for 1:30 to 2:00,
  7. Flip the steak and sear the other side, reducing the temperature to medium around half way,
  8. Add the butter and allow it to melt, then add the rosemary sprig to the butter,
  9. Begin basting the top side of the steak, then flip it over after around 30 seconds,
  10. Baste the other side of the steak for around 30 seconds, then flip and repeat the process once,
  11. Remove the steak and let it rest on a plate for 5 minutes,
  12. Remove the rosemary sprig,
  13. Increase the temperature to high again and add the piece of bread,
  14. Allow the first side to toast, then flip and toast the other side,
  15. Serve.
